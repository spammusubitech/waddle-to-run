# Privacy Policy for Waddle to Run

Last updated: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spam Musubi Technologies LLC handles information when You use Waddle to Run. References to Waddle to Run may include the `goose-running` app, public pages, support materials, or similar names used for the Service.

Waddle to Run helps people begin or return to walking and running with adaptive plans, GPS run tracking, route cards, reminders, progress, and a playful goose companion. Most plan, route, run, and preference information is stored on Your Device and, when available under Your Apple and iCloud settings, in Your private iCloud database.

## Information We Handle

### Information You Provide

Depending on the features You use, You may provide:

- Onboarding and plan choices, such as age range, prior running experience, current jogging capacity, goals, preferred running location, perceived blockers, difficulty preference, preferred run days, and weekly goal.
- Settings and preferences, such as distance units, audio and interval cues, reminders, route privacy mode, Apple Health saving, and commentary choices.
- Run reflections, including an optional mood, feeling, or note after a run.
- Routes You draw or save and names You give them.
- Support messages, email address, and other contact details if You contact Us.
- Purchase-related choices, such as which Subscription You select, as processed through the Application Store and Our purchase provider.

You are not required to create a Waddle to Run account to use the current iOS Application.

### Location, Route, and Run Information

When You start an outdoor run or walk and grant location permission, the Application may process precise location samples to:

- Draw and save Your route.
- Estimate distance, current speed, pace, moving time, paused time, and automatic pauses.
- Filter inaccurate or implausible GPS samples.
- Compare a recorded activity with a route You planned.
- Create route history, route cards, progress summaries, and a shareable route postcard.

A saved run may include coordinates, timestamps, horizontal accuracy, start and end times, total and moving duration, distance, average pace, maximum detected speed, pause information, interval completion, mission or plan association, progress rewards, route-shape descriptions, and optional reflections.

The current location service requests permission when You begin tracking and uses location for active run and walk recording. Permission choices are controlled through Your Device settings. If location is unavailable or denied, the Application may offer a demo or timer-based experience instead of recording Your actual route.

The route privacy setting changes how routes are displayed on route cards and share images. For example, it can hide or trim the visible start and end of a route. The full recorded route may remain in the Application’s saved run data unless You delete the run or its data.

### Plans, Progress, and App Preferences

The Application may store or calculate:

- Training plans, planned runs, scheduled dates, movement intervals, duration or distance targets, and completed-plan links.
- Weekly goals, plan progress, run history, streaks, experience points, badges, unlocked or equipped goose items, and similar progress information.
- Difficulty adjustments, including whether a run felt too hard, whether an easier option was used, and the next-run adjustment You select.
- Reminder schedules and whether reminders are enabled.
- Application settings and feature-access state.

This information is used to personalize the plan and experience, show progress, schedule reminders, and preserve Your choices.

### Apple Health

If You turn on “Save runs to Apple Health” and grant permission, Waddle to Run may write a completed walking or running workout to Apple Health. The workout may include activity type, start and end time, duration, and distance. Demo-location activities are not written with a route-derived distance.

The current Application does not request permission to read Health data. We do not use Health information for advertising, marketing, or unrelated data mining.

Health data is stored and managed by Apple Health on Your Device and through Apple services according to Your settings and Apple’s policies. You can review or revoke Waddle to Run’s Health access in the Health app or Device settings.

### Notifications and Audio Cues

If notification permission is granted, the Application can schedule local reminders for planned runs. Reminder scheduling and delivery are handled by Your Device and Apple notification services. The current build uses local plan reminders and is not designed to send marketing push notifications.

If audio cues are enabled, the Application may use on-device speech synthesis and haptics for run, walk, and interval prompts. The Application is not designed to record microphone audio.

### Route Postcards and Sharing

You can create a route postcard image and choose to share it through the iOS share sheet. Sharing is user-initiated. The image may include a displayed route, distance, duration, pace, mission information, and other visible recap details.

When You choose a destination in the share sheet, Apple and the selected application or recipient may process the shared image under their own terms and privacy policies. Waddle to Run does not automatically publish route postcards to a public feed.

Review the postcard and Your route privacy setting before sharing. Hiding start and end points reduces visible detail but does not guarantee that a route cannot reveal a sensitive place.

### Purchases and Subscriptions

If You make an In-app Purchase or purchase a Subscription, payment is processed by the Application Store. We do not receive or store Your full payment card number, billing-card security code, or full Application Store account credentials.

The Application uses RevenueCat to manage offerings, purchases, Subscription status, entitlements, purchase restoration, and related diagnostics. RevenueCat may process a pseudonymous app user identifier, Application Store transaction and receipt information, product and entitlement information, Device and app metadata, IP address, and related diagnostics needed to provide purchase services.

### Apple, iCloud, Maps, and Diagnostics

The Application uses Apple frameworks and services, including SwiftData, iCloud and CloudKit, MapKit, Core Location, HealthKit, local notifications, and Application Store services.

Depending on Your Device, Apple account, iCloud, backup, and Application settings:

- Plans, scheduled runs, routes, run history, progress, preferences, and similar Application data may sync through Your private iCloud database.
- Application data may be included in Device or iCloud backups.
- Apple may process map requests, location permissions, Health data, notifications, purchase records, crash reports, performance diagnostics, and App Store analytics under Apple’s policies.

We do not operate a separate server that stores Your raw GPS route history. The current Application does not include a third-party advertising SDK. The PostHog software framework is bundled as a project dependency, but the current Application code does not configure PostHog or send product-analytics events to it. If We enable third-party analytics in a future version, We will update this Privacy Policy and make any required disclosures or consent controls available.

## How Information Is Stored

Plans, scheduled runs, routes, run history, progress, reflections, preferences, and settings are primarily stored using storage provided by iOS on Your Device. When iCloud is available and configured for the Application, this information may sync through Apple CloudKit to Your private iCloud database.

We do not have Your Apple ID password and generally cannot directly access information stored only on Your Device or in Your private iCloud database.

Apple Health workouts are stored in HealthKit. Purchase and entitlement records are handled by Apple and RevenueCat. Support correspondence is stored through the communication service You use to contact Us and Our business systems.

## Retention

Information stored in the Application remains on Your Device or in Your private iCloud database until You delete relevant records, erase Application data through an available control, delete the Application, or manage the data through Apple services, subject to backup and sync behavior.

Deleting a run or planned route through the Application removes that record from the active Application data store and may synchronize the deletion through CloudKit. Copies may remain temporarily in backups, caches, or Apple systems according to Apple’s retention practices.

Apple Health workouts remain in Apple Health until You delete them there. Deleting a run from Waddle to Run does not necessarily delete a workout previously saved to Apple Health.

Support messages and related correspondence may be retained as long as needed to respond to You, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce Our agreements.

Purchase and entitlement records are retained by the Application Store and RevenueCat according to their policies and as needed to provide the Service, restore purchases, comply with law, and prevent fraud.

## Your Choices and Deleting Information

You can:

- Decline or revoke location, Health, and notification permissions in Your Device settings.
- Turn off reminders, audio cues, Apple Health saving, or other optional features in the Application.
- Delete individual saved routes where the Application provides that control.
- Delete Application data by deleting records through available controls or deleting the Application, subject to iCloud sync and backup behavior.
- Delete workouts written by Waddle to Run from the Apple Health app.
- Manage iCloud and backup behavior through Your Apple account and Device settings.
- Manage or cancel Subscriptions through Your Application Store account settings.

Deleting the Application may not delete information stored by Apple or RevenueCat, including Application Store purchase history, active Subscriptions, Health workouts, iCloud data, backups, diagnostics, or entitlement records.

You may contact Us to request access, correction, or deletion of Personal Data You have provided directly to Us. Because much of the Service’s data is stored on Your Device or in Your private Apple services, We may not possess or be able to retrieve it. We may need to retain certain information when required or permitted by law.

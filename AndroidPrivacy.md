# Privacy Policy — Waddle: Beginner Running Coach for Android

Last updated: September 9, 2026. Version: android-2026-09-09.1.

Spam Musubi Technologies LLC provides Waddle: Beginner Running Coach (also called Waddle). This policy describes the Android application, package `com.spammusubi.gooserunning`. The company can be contacted at contact@spammusubitech.com or 3400 Cottage Way, Ste G2 #11929, Sacramento, California 95825, United States.

## Information stored on your device

Waddle stores your running experience, age range, jogging capacity, goals, preferred locations, blockers, training days, difficulty, generated plans, schedule, preferences, and progress on your device. It also stores completed activities, timestamps, duration, distance, route points, optional mood and effort reflections, notes, reward selections, and saved geographic routes and route sketches. No Waddle account is required.

The app records your acceptance of its terms, including document URLs, agreement version and copy hash, time, app version, language, and whether Premium access was verified. These records stay with the local app data.

## Location

Outdoor recording requests precise location when you choose to start an outdoor activity. Location points and timestamps are used to estimate distance and pace, record a route, detect pauses, and make route postcards. Location continues during an active foreground workout when the screen is locked or the app is minimized; a persistent notification provides workout controls. Tracking stops when you finish or discard the activity. Indoor/timer sessions do not need location.

GPS estimates can be inaccurate. Route privacy settings affect postcard display, not the underlying locally saved route. The default removes points within 200 meters of the start and end from a postcard. You can hide the route entirely, and should review the displayed route before sharing. Deleting a run removes its route from the app's saved history.

The Android app does not upload raw location, routes, fitness profiles, moods, or notes to RevenueCat or an app-operated server.

## Street maps and place searches

Street maps load only when you choose to load a map or open a street-map view. OpenStreetMap receives your IP address, the requested map tile areas, and an application-identifying user agent. Requested areas can reveal the approximate location you are viewing. Waddle caches map tiles on your device and does not upload your drawn route, notes, mood, or fitness profile to OpenStreetMap. Map imagery is provided by OpenStreetMap contributors under its attribution requirements; see the [OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

When you search for a place, the search text is sent to the geocoding provider available through your Android device. Its handling is subject to that provider’s terms and privacy policy. Search is optional; you can move the map manually. Drawn distances are estimates along your drawing, not road routing or safety advice.

## Health Connect

If you enable Health Connect saving and grant permission, Waddle writes completed exercise sessions and distance records to Health Connect. It requests no health-data read permissions. Health information is used only for the workout feature you request; it is not used for advertising, data brokerage, marketing, or unrelated analysis.

You can revoke permission or delete records through Health Connect. Turning off saving does not remove past records. Deleting an activity that Waddle previously saved to Health Connect also attempts to remove the corresponding records; if permission is unavailable, the app asks you to restore access before completing that deletion. Deleting all local app data does not delete existing Health Connect records.

Waddle's use of Health Connect information is limited to providing or improving its user-facing health and fitness functionality, consistent with the Health Connect permissions policy and limited-use requirements.

## Purchases

Google Play handles payment, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, and refunds. We do not receive full card numbers, card security codes, or your Google account password. RevenueCat verifies Premium access and restores purchases. It processes a pseudonymous app user ID, product and transaction/purchase-token information, subscription status, app/device metadata, IP address, and purchase diagnostics. Google Play can send renewal, cancellation, and refund notifications to RevenueCat through Google Cloud Pub/Sub.

See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

A complimentary access pass issued by support is verified and stored on your device. It does not start a subscription or charge your Google Play account. Passes are excluded from backup exports and removed when you remove the pass or delete local app data.

## Optional product analytics

Product analytics are off by default. If you enable them in Settings, PostHog receives a pseudonymous application identifier, app and device metadata, IP address, selected screen or feature names, and purchase-flow events such as product selection, checkout success or cancellation, and restore results. Waddle uses these events to understand feature usage and evaluate paywall variants in its Waddle analytics project.

Waddle does not send route coordinates, location trails, fitness profile answers, workout measurements, notes, moods, or Health Connect records to PostHog. Session replay, automatic screen capture, automatic interaction capture, deep-link capture, and automatic error capture are disabled. No advertising SDK is included. Turning analytics off stops subsequent product event capture. See [PostHog’s privacy notice](https://posthog.com/privacy).

## Notifications and audio

Optional workout reminders and trial-renewal reminders are scheduled locally by Android. Trial reminders use the expiration date most recently verified by RevenueCat and are canceled when the app receives a status showing that the trial is no longer active or will not renew. A reminder does not cancel a subscription. Their timing depends on notification permission, device power settings, and the operating system. The active-workout notification lets you pause or finish recording. Optional speech synthesis and vibration provide interval cues. The app does not record microphone audio.

## Sharing, backups, and deletion

When you share a postcard, export a six-second replay video, or export a backup, Android sends the chosen file to the app or destination you select. A full JSON backup contains your saved profile, routes, history, notes, settings, and agreement record. Keep it private. A route privacy option on postcards does not redact the full backup. The receiving service's policies govern files you choose to send there.

Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer of Waddle's data are disabled. You can explicitly export a backup and restore a compatible backup on another device. Restoring replaces local app data after confirmation and does not grant Premium, notification permission, or Health Connect permission.

You can delete individual runs or delete all local data in Settings. Uninstalling also removes local app storage. Copies you exported, sent to someone, or saved in another application remain there until you remove them. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription or erase purchase records held by Google Play or RevenueCat.

## Support, retention, and security

If you contact us, we process your message, email address, and any information you choose to include to provide support. Do not send a full route backup unless it is needed for your support request. Support records and purchase records may be retained for operational, fraud-prevention, accounting, and legal obligations. Local records remain until you delete them or remove the application.

The app uses private Android app storage, encrypted HTTPS purchase connections, and atomic local writes. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

## Children and your choices

Waddle is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information. Use the app's export and deletion controls for local records, or contact us for information held by the company or its service providers. We do not sell your personal information or use health or location information for targeted advertising.

## Changes

We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. The published version will state its effective date. Contact contact@spammusubitech.com with privacy questions.
